WHAT IS AN IPO?
An Initial Public Offering is the first time a private company sells shares to public investors. A share is a small ownership stake. Request shares at the offering price, or buy after listing at the market price.
02
VERIFY THE OFFERING

The debut listing is Thursday, Nov 19. Dates can still change, so confirm the prospectus, final price, deadlines and brokerage access before acting.
03
HOW DOES THE IPO LOTTERY WORK?
People ask for more shares than actually exist, so brokers run a lottery. For example, if a broker receives 10,000 shares and customers request 100,000, some requests go unfilled. Allocation can be random, proportional or tier-based. You may get all, some or none of the shares you request, but you can still buy after the debut listing.
  • Debut listing: Thursday, Nov 19. The market opens at 7:30 AM Boise time, and IPO shares usually start trading in the late morning.
  • Pre-Order: Queue a limit order before the first trade, so it fills only at or below your price. At Chase, you can queue one starting at 5 AM Boise time on listing day.
