WHAT IS AN IPO?
An Initial Public Offering is the first time a private company sells shares to public investors. A share is a small ownership stake. Request shares at the offering price, or buy after listing at the market price.
02

HOW DOES THE IPO LOTTERY WORK?
People ask for more shares than actually exist, so brokers run an IPO lottery. For example, if a broker receives 10,000 shares and its customers request 100,000 shares collectively, some requests go unfilled. Allocation can be random, proportional or tier-based. You may get all, some or none of the shares you request, but you can still buy after the debut listing.
  • Pre-Order: 5:00 AM Nov 19 Thu, J.P. Morgan lets you queue a limit order. It only buys at or below your price.
  • Debut listing: the market opens 7:30 AM, but ANT trades later, likely 8:00 AM–12:00 PM. All times Boise.
