# Weekly Digest: Portfolio Puffer's weekly showcase, first edition made for Extroid (2026-11-20)

Astra, 2026-11-20: "I think this can be called the weekly digest, and its part of portfolio puffer. it showcases the things I worked on this week and really it shows the new things mostly, but also the most ingenuitive things, or the things I request, make it a feature of the portfolio puffer as a tool".

- New page at `findastra.github.io/weekly-digest/`: a numbered list, and each item is a picture, its link, then a description, with numbered subsections where an item needs them, the way the Anthropic IPO guide numbers its sections.
- Same colors and fonts as the Anthropic IPO guide (fonts loaded from `../anthropic-ipo/fonts/`). Behind it, AI Discord Presence's two galaxies spin slowly. They turn clockwise, so their arms trail, and they stop when the system asks for less motion.
- Editions are data: `editions/weekly-digest-YYYYMMDD.js`. `index.html` draws the newest one, and `?edition=YYYYMMDD` draws an older one. Only ids listed in `EDITIONS` load.
- This first edition (Nov 14–20, 2026) was made for Extroid. It has ten items: The Cooling Tower first ("you're playing here"), then Ghost Protocol's three networks with the white rabbit, Abliteration in 3D, Astra's PetOS, the pet chat seats, the Anthropic IPO guide, Papermon, AI Discord Presence, Liminal Doors and the bio.
