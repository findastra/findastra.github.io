# Spoonfed: a real spoon in the logo

Astra, 2026-11-05: "i wanted it to look like the real image of a spoon, not a made up coded one".

Replaced `spoonfed/` again with a new web build of `findastra/spoonfed` at 740169e (branch `claude/sugarcube-pet-well-20261103`, draft PR #9). The logo's drawn spoon is now a photo of a real spoon, with Spoonfed Sugarcube sitting in its bowl and its handle running under the word. The long names on phones from `spoonfed-long-names-20261104.md` are in this build too.

What is public: the built page, its script, fonts and the spoon picture. No keys or personal details (checked: no email, no PC path).

Validation: the same build served at `/spoonfed/` on this PC, at phone size (375 × 812) and computer size, in headless Edge and the browser pane: the spoon shows under Spoonfed Sugarcube and the word, and the console shows no errors. Not yet checked on a real phone.
