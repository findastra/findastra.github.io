Replaced `spoonfed/` with a new web build of `findastra/spoonfed` at `main` 41d23a9 (spoonfed#19, merged with Astra's OK). What changes:

- **The preview ribbon.** The pick card and the big card show Google's photos side by side in one strip that slides freely. Every photo keeps its own shape at the same height.
- **Row cards.** Each card shows the spot's sharpest, biggest food photo. The photo area has the ribbon's shape, every card is the same height, and the row scrolls at half the old speed.
- **No photos with writing added.** Logos, menus, adverts and other graphics with text are left out everywhere.
- **One screen.** No vertical scroll bar on any window that isn't an upright phone or tablet. Button rows never wrap.
- **Hover.** A thicker ring, even on every side, on the cards and the Feed me panel. No drop shadows anywhere.
- **Spacing.** The pick card's buttons sit halfway between its facts and its bottom edge, and the row cards' text sits halfway between the photo and the bottom.
- **Footer.** Georgia, with its line in the buttons' border colour.
