# Spoonfed: veggie means real vegetarian meals, a green Happy Cow button, Bar renamed Alcohol

Astra, 2026-11-14:

- "I had a friend mention that the veggie thing didn't work. That it was showing up like Culver's was veggie."
- "ice cream places and cocktail bars are not considered veggie, even if they do offer non-meat. Like it's got to have vegetarian meals."
- "just put a Happy Cow button on it that's green"
- "the one that says bar is like serves alcohol ... it should be like alcohol or something"

Replaced `spoonfed/` with a new web build of `findastra/spoonfed` at `main` fbf34c7 (spoonfed#24, merged with Astra's OK). What changes:

- **Veggie means real vegetarian meals.** A vegetarian or vegan kitchen always counts. Otherwise only a sit-down kitchen whose menus usually have vegetarian meals (Indian, Thai, Mediterranean, Italian, Mexican and similar) that Google says serves vegetarian food. Fast food, burgers, ice cream, dessert, coffee and bars never count.
- **Green Happy Cow button** with a leaf on veggie cards. It opens Happy Cow's vegetarian map at the spot's address. Nothing is read from Happy Cow.
- **Bar is now Alcohol**, with a wine glass: spots that serve beer, wine or cocktails.

What is public: the built page and its script. No key, email or PC path is in the files (checked). The deploy adds the Google key as before.

By Astra.
